- **Fixed: a list rebuild could be killed at once every time, or pause searches until a restart.** After a rebuild that was stopped for hanging, or one cut off by a crash or a restart, the next rebuild was often killed within seconds as "stalled", and so was every one after it, so the list never refreshed. And a rebuild that got stuck right at its start, for example on a network drive that stopped answering, kept searches and file requests paused until you restarted the bot. Both are fixed: the rebuild reports from its very first step and is ended like any other stuck rebuild, and an old run's report no longer counts against a new one.

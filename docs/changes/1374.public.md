- **New: read-only console tokens.** `pair <name> <version> readonly` at the admin console gives a script a token that can look at the bot (status, queue, sends, library, bans, the feed) and change nothing: no bans, queue changes, rebuilds, settings or shutdown, and it cannot be unlocked. It also never takes the console over from you: while you are logged in with your password or a full token, a read-only login is turned away until the console is free. Handy for a stats script or a status widget that you would rather not trust with everything.

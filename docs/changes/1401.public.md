- **New: how long DCCore Chat's history is kept, and how big it may grow.** Two settings under **Settings → Private messages** on the dashboard, and on the control panel's mIRC side (*Chat* page): *Keep the chat history in mIRC for* (100 days out of the box) and *Largest chat history in mIRC* (100 MB). 0 is no limit. The bot sends them to `dccore.mrc`, which deletes the oldest months of its history files by them.

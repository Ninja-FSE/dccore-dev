- **New (mIRC): the settings window is redrawn, and can use DCX.** Every section sits in a titled frame with its labels above the fields, in a window that fits a 1080p screen. `dcx.dll` (DCX, https://github.com/twig/dcxdll, BSD-3-Clause) now ships in `scripts/mirc/dcx/`, unmodified, with Microsoft's `WebView2Loader.dll` and both licences. With it the window is a **control panel**: it opens on a start page with two choices, **Bot** (the bot's settings, six tabs with their pages listed on the left) and **mIRC** (this mIRC's own: the @DCCore window, its look, the link to the bot, chat - what the Options dialog held), with a back arrow at the top left; every line of the window is drawn in one dark grey with rounded corners, the page's name is above the page, and every table has columns - the channels, the lists and the channel bindings, the bans: select a row and use the buttons under it. Lists & channels is two tables now, the lists with their folders and the channel bindings, each with its own Change and Remove. One menu entry, **Control Panel...**, replaces *Script Settings...* and *Bot Settings...*; `/dccore options` still opens this mIRC's own settings. Whenever DCX cannot be used - a 64-bit or pre-7.45 mIRC, the folder not copied, or the option *Use the built-in settings window, not DCX* switched on - the same pages open with mIRC's own controls. `/dccore dcx` says which applies, and `/dccore dcx check` tests DCX in your mIRC. Copy the `dcx` folder beside `dccore.mrc`, update `dccore.mrc` to 1.20.0 and reload it with `/reload -rs dccore.mrc`.

- **Fixed: a send could be stopped as slow after *Packet size* was lowered.** With *Max upload speed* and *Stop sends slower than* (`SLOW_SEND_MIN_KB`) set, lowering *Packet size* (`DCC_BLOCK_SIZE`) while sends were running could leave the sends started afterwards with less than their share of the upload speed, and one of them could be stopped as too slow although only the cap held it back. Every running send now uses the new packet size from its next packet on.

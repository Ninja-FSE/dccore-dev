- **New (mIRC): DCCore Chat keeps a history, on by default.** Every line the chat window shows, private conversations included, is written as plain text to mIRC's *log* folder (`//echo -a $logdir`), in `DCCore-Chat\`: a file per channel and per private conversation for each month, kept for as long and up to the size the bot's settings say (100 days and 100 MB out of the box, #1401). The window says where when it opens, and shows the last 200 lines again, so closing it or restarting mIRC loses nothing. A line that arrives while you are in another window, or while mIRC is in the background, is counted in the chat window's title, lights its button, and makes mIRC flash in the taskbar while it is in the background. Untick *Keep a history file* or *Flash on a new line* in the window's right-click menu to turn either off; the menu also opens the folder. Reload `dccore.mrc` with `/reload -rs dccore.mrc`.

- **Changed: one place for your folders, and one Save.** *Settings > Your list* no longer has a separate *Music directory* field: your folders are the rows at the top, and a bot with one folder is a single-folder bot. If you never saved a folder list, your Music directory is the first row. The page's **Save** button (bottom right) now saves the folders as well as the settings; the *Save folders* and *Save lists* buttons are gone. Saving with no folders at all is refused, since a bot with no folders has nothing to share. Rebuild the list from *Tools* after changing folders, as before. In the mIRC settings window the *Music directory* field is gone too.

- **Fixed: a list rebuild kept running after you stopped the bot, and blocked the next one.** Stopped from *Tools > Stop* (or Ctrl-C) during a rebuild, the bot ended but the rebuild scanned on, and after a restart *Update list* said "Another list rebuild is already running". Now the bot asks it to stop on the way out, and a rebuild whose bot has gone (a crash, a closed window) stops by itself within seconds. A stopped rebuild publishes nothing: your current list stays in use.

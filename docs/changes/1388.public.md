- **New: stop a send that crawls while others wait.** *Stop sends slower than* (`SLOW_SEND_MIN_KB`, in KB/s) stops the slowest send when its downloader has received less than that over the last minute, but only while every slot is taken and someone is waiting for one, and only while another of your sends is reaching that speed - so a full uplink, a slow disk or your own *Max upload speed* never gets a send stopped, and with one slot it never fires. Never in a send's first minute. The slot goes to the next in line. A file that was waiting in the queue stays queued: it counts as a failed try against *Max send failures*, the person goes to the back and is told why, and their client can resume it later. A file sent straight away without waiting is dropped, and they ask for it again. Set it on the Settings page under *Sharing & queue*, or in the mIRC settings window. 0, off, out of the box.

- **New: someone whose downloads keep failing is paused until midnight.** *Failed sends in a day before a nick is paused* (`DAILY_SEND_FAILS_LIMIT`) stops a downloader whose client never accepts your offers, sits behind a firewall, or drops every transfer, from holding your slots all day: once their sends have failed that many times since midnight, the files left in their queue are removed and new requests are refused until midnight, with a notice telling them to check their client. A transfer the downloader closes or cancels in their own client before it finishes counts as a failure, since the bot cannot tell it from a client that drops every send. Taking something out of the queue (`packcancel`, `@<bot>-remove`) does not count, nor does a failure on your side (a file that vanished, a network share that stopped answering), and admins are never paused. Offers nobody connects to stop counting once offers to several downloaders in a row go unanswered with no send connecting in between, until a send connects again, so a wrong address or a lost port forward on your side does not pause everybody. Before turning it on, know that someone who keeps connecting to your DCC ports can get in ahead of a downloader and make their sends fail, which with this limit can get them paused: set it high, or leave it off where that is a concern. The console's `status` shows who is paused, and `unpause <nick>` lifts it. 0, the default, is off; set it on the Settings page under *Transfers* or in the mIRC settings window.

- **New: a maximum upload and download speed.** *Max upload speed* (`MAX_UPLOAD_KB`) caps all your sends together, and *Max download speed* (`MAX_DOWNLOAD_KB`) everything the bot fetches: lists, automatic grabs, files and folders from other bots. Both are in KB/s and shared by every transfer going that way, so three sends under 600 get about 200 each. A change applies at once, to transfers already running too. Set them on the Settings page under *Transfers*, or in the mIRC settings window. Both are 0, no limit, out of the box, so nothing changes until you set them.
